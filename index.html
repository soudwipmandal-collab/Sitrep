PS Daily Sitrep Beta

Fill in today's figures for your station and save. Pick the date of incident. The report for a date can be filled in and edited from 00:00 on that date until 07:00 the next morning (e.g. 10.10.2026 is open from 00:00 on 10.10.2026 to 07:00 on 11.10.2026). Future dates are blocked and, after 07:00 the next morning, the report is read-only.
